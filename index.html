DESCRIPTION

Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod

The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (OSSP) provides administrative, secretarial, legislative, and technical support services to the Sangguniang Panlungsod in the performance of its functions as the legislative, deliberative, and policy-making body of the City Government.

◈

Mission

To provide efficient, responsive, transparent, and professional legislative, secretariat, administrative, and technical support services to the Sangguniang Panlungsod, ensuring the timely and accurate processing, recording, preservation, and accessibility of legislative documents and proceedings.

◉

Vision

A modern, accountable, and service-oriented Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod that upholds excellence in legislative support, promotes transparency and good governance, and continuously improves its systems and services for the people of Quezon City.

LEGAL BASES

Legal Foundations

SERVICE PLEDGE

Our Commitment to the Public

In fulfilling its mandate, the OSSP is committed to delivering quality public service guided by the following principles:

01

Accessibility

We make information, assistance, and services accessible to the public and authorized stakeholders.

02

Timeliness

We strive to process requests, documents, and legislative matters promptly and efficiently.

03

Transparency

We promote openness and provide clear and reliable information within the bounds of applicable laws and policies.

04

Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions, decisions, records, and services.

05

Professionalism

We uphold competence, integrity, courtesy, and respect in serving the Sangguniang Panlungsod and the public.

06

Continuous Improvement

We continuously improve our systems, processes, and services to better respond to the needs of the City.

The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod remains committed to serving Quezon City with integrity, excellence, and dedication, while supporting effective legislative governance and responsive public service.
